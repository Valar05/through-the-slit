# Known limitations — v1.0.0-rc1

- The battlefield is deliberately visual and is not claimed to be fully
  playable without sight. Menus, settings, status regions, reduced motion,
  reduced flashes, contrast, large text, touch-width, focus pause, and separate
  audio controls are implemented; nonvisual play requires a separate accepted
  design and end-to-end test.
- WebGL is required. Unsupported or disabled graphics contexts fail closed with
  an observation-port message instead of presenting a counterfeit battlefield.
- Device-local settings do not roam between browsers or devices.
- One severe terrain seed can still punish the infantry formation heavily. This
  is accepted tactical variance, not proof that every seed is equally forgiving.
- The release contains one completed three-generation inheritance proof,
  Martyr's Winch. Other grafts are not represented as completed hereditary
  lineages.
- The public GitHub source and the live Site are distinct evidence surfaces.
  Neither proves the other without an exact source/deployment comparison.
