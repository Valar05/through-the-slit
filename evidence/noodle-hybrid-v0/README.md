# Noodle Hybrid v0 — Through the Slit

Status: **PROTOTYPED IN REPOSITORY / NOT YET RUNTIME-INTEGRATED / NOT USER-KEPT**

This packet is the first concrete proof for the commissioned hybrid pixel + vector animation language. It does **not** replace the live art pipeline and it does **not** claim phone acceptance.

## Authority recovered before design

The prototype is derived from existing project evidence rather than a free-form style invention:

- Noodle Queen judgment law: clean expressive vector paths, line-weight variation, silhouette, facial acting, living curls/filaments, editorial still composition, motion with cause, continuity, economy, accessibility, and explicit rejection of abstraction replacing the subject.
- Noodle Queen failure evidence: decorative/repetitive output without joy or semantic purpose is rejected; accessibility must be proven on the actual access path and must improve rather than flatten the work.
- Through the Slit v39 handoff: first-person slit camera, twin-tread authority, painted sprite battlefield language, nearest-neighbor texture behavior, grounded sprites sharing the real 3D scene/depth buffer, phone-short-landscape as a controlling gate, and no laundering repository/test evidence into Pixel acceptance.
- Vow Motion Lab: authored extremes, selective tween, anticipation compression, force-directed stretch/shear, atlas-safe morph, explicit ownership of moving parts, contact lock/hit-stop, shared force response, exact recovery, and accessibility variants that preserve chronology/causality.

## The hybrid representation

One gameplay specimen is represented by three coupled layers:

1. **Vector master / rig** — identity, silhouette, attachment points, force paths, reusable poses, deformation envelopes, and scalable authoring.
2. **Pixel witness layer** — texture quantization, grit, wounds, edge breakup, muzzle/impact debris, local material noise, damage states, and dataset-friendly raster outputs. It never changes identity or contact ownership.
3. **Semantic state** — pose name, view angle, force vector, material, damage, accessibility mode, and deterministic seed. Both render layers consume the same state.

This means the vector layer can remain clean and riggable while the pixel layer can stay crunchy and authored. The two cannot disagree about anatomy, contact, force, or timing.

## Animation law

Animation is not “more frames.” Each action is a sentence of authored extremes:

`guard → load → apex → contact/embed → extraction/rebound → recovery`

Selective tween connects those extremes. Channels may step, hold, overshoot, or snap independently. Contact is a punctuation event shared by body, weapon, target, debris, camera pressure, and pixel treatment.

The first proof deliberately demonstrates:

- first-person weapon/body presentation;
- a readable enemy silhouette;
- vector deformation driven by force;
- pixel texture/damage/effect witnesses driven by the same force;
- deterministic variants;
- reduced-motion and reduced-flash variants that preserve causality;
- raster-friendly canonical states suitable for a future curated LoRA/Velora corpus.

## LoRA / Velora corpus rule

Training truth is not whatever looked amusing once. A specimen becomes `TRAINING_ELIGIBLE` only after:

- a stable semantic label exists;
- canonical pose and view are declared;
- source master and derived raster are distinguishable;
- the same semantic state regenerates deterministically;
- identity/contact invariants survive variation;
- negative fixtures exist for known bad traits;
- Noodle Queen/taste + accessibility judgment has a recorded outcome;
- the user has not rejected the lineage.

The sample corpus in this directory is therefore **schema proof only**, not accepted training data.

## Expansion grammar

Infinite expansion comes from compatible primitives, not an infinite pile of bespoke sprites:

- anatomy atoms: torso mass, limb/appendage, eye/sensor, maw/tool interface, tissue cable/noodle;
- equipment atoms: barrel, shovel/blade, shield/scute, tendon-feed, organ socket;
- material atoms: wet tissue, scar, bone/ivory, cloth, mud, steel, chitin;
- pose atoms: guard, load, brace, lunge, fire, recoil, stagger, death, recover;
- view atoms: frontal, quarter-left/right, profile, rear-quarter;
- consequence atoms: dirt, blood, sparks, steam, spores, tendon snap, armor chip;
- accessibility atoms: motion amplitude, flash intensity, contrast witness, silhouette witness.

Compatibility rules determine which atoms can join. Mutation axes change proportions, curvature, spacing, texture density, damage, and timing without changing semantic ownership.
